# Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.

<details>
<summary>Why it matters</summary>
In today's connected world, cybersecurity is essential for protecting sensitive information and ensuring business continuity.
</details>

---

## Common Threats

- **Phishing**: Trick emails that look real
- **Malware**: Harmful software that can damage or steal data
- **Ransomware**: Locks you out until you pay up

---

## Best Practices

1. Use strong, unique passwords.
2. Enable two-factor authentication.
3. Avoid clicking suspicious links or attachments.

<details>
<summary>More about strong passwords</summary>
Use a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols. Avoid dictionary words or reused credentials.
</details>

---

## Case Study: Acme Corp

In 2021, Acme Corp suffered a major breach due to an employee falling for a phishing attack.

- **Result**: 10,000 records exposed
- **Lesson**: Security awareness is crucial.

---

## Summary

Cybersecurity is everyone’s responsibility. Stay alert, use best practices, and never stop learning.
